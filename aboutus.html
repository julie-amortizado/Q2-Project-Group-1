About Us

Advancing The Montessori Way

At OBMC, we are dedicated to advancing the Montessori way by providing an environment where every child can explore, learn, and grow at their own pace. We nurture independence, curiosity, and a love for learning, while fostering responsibility, respect, and empathy. Our hands-on, experiential approach encourages self-directed exploration and critical thinking, helping students develop confidence, problem-solving skills, and a lifelong passion for knowledge. By following Dr. Montessori’s vision, OBMC prepares children not only academically, but also socially and emotionally, to thrive in a constantly changing world.

Founders and History

We honor the vision and dedication of Dr. Preciosa S. Soliven, who founded the first O.B. Montessori School in Manila in 1966. Guided by her passion for Montessori education, the school began as a small institution and has grown into a respected network of campuses, providing holistic, child-centered learning across the Philippines. Through decades of commitment to excellence, OBMC has nurtured thousands of students, staying true to Dr. Soliven’s mission of fostering independence, curiosity, and lifelong learning, while shaping responsible and compassionate individuals ready to make a positive impact in the world.

Leadership

At OBMC, we are guided by visionary leadership that upholds the principles of Montessori education while driving innovation and excellence across all our campuses. Our leaders are dedicated to fostering a nurturing and inspiring environment for both students and educators, ensuring that every child receives a holistic, well-rounded education. By combining educational expertise with a commitment to values, integrity, and community engagement, OBMC’s leadership continues to shape a school culture that empowers children to become independent, confident, and compassionate individuals ready to thrive in a complex and ever-changing world.
